# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming project community. Participation should improve the software and the experience of the people building and operating it.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate with empathy and professional respect;
- critique ideas and evidence rather than people;
- state uncertainty and correct mistakes openly;
- respect privacy, credentials, data ownership, and provider terms;
- keep discussions relevant and actionable; and
- help maintain an environment where newcomers can ask good-faith questions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing private communications, deliberate credential exposure, sustained disruption, personal attacks, and encouraging abuse of provider accounts or platform restrictions.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project chat used for collaboration, and public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using contact information on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive incident details publicly. Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, limit participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid disclosing more personal information than necessary.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy is informed by common open-source community standards. It may be replaced by a formally versioned community covenant as the maintainer team grows.
